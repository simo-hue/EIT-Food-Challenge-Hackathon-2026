FridgeMate

Your old fridge finally knows who’s coming and going.

A portable camera for any fridge

Try the demo One minute, in your browser

The problem

Every home throws away around €500 of food a year.

It gets pushed to the back of the fridge, forgotten, and found too late.

69

kg of food thrown away per person, every year, in EU households.

How it works

A portable camera that makes any fridge smart.

  1. 01

    Portable

    It clips on top of any fridge. Unclip it and take it to your next one.

  2. 02

    In and out

    One narrow camera watches the doorway and logs every item going in and out, so nothing gets lost at the back.

  3. 03

    On-device AI

    Recognition runs on the device itself.

  4. 04

    No video upload

    No video leaves the fridge.

The app

Know what you have. Cook it before it goes.

  • Fridge

    Everything you have, sorted by what expires first, with a warning before food goes off.

  • Cook

    Meals from what is already in the fridge, starting with what expires soonest.

  • Stats

    What you saved, in money and in food, with streaks for zero-waste weeks.

  • Shared

    Name tags for shared fridges: mine, or free for everyone.
